Tracker FAQ

How often is this tracker updated?

We re-check each status against FDA's own pages and add any change to the What-changed log with its date and source. Each guide shows when its status was last checked.

Does a PCAC vote mean a peptide is approved or compoundable?

No. Advisory committee votes are recommendations. FDA hasn't added any of the July 2026 peptides to the 503A list (FDA 503A list).
