What the FDA has actually said about peptide sellers, quoted word for word and linked to fda.gov. No rumors, no screenshots.

FDA warning letters

Letters FDA sent to online sellers of peptide products. A warning letter is FDA's notice of violations it observed; it isn't a court finding.

  1. Aug 24, 2026 · TXP Innovations LLC dba Tex Peptides
    Products named: Semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide, SS-31, tesamorelin, PT-141, bacteriostatic water

    "These products are especially concerning from a public health perspective because injectable drug products can pose risks of serious harm to users."

    FDA warning letter

  2. Aug 24, 2026 · Royal Peptides LLC
    Products named: Tirzepatide, semaglutide, retatrutide, SS-31, PT-141, tesamorelin, "BIMORELIN"

    "…are unapproved new drugs under section 505(a) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FD&C Act)"

    FDA warning letter

  3. Mar 31, 2026 · Gram Peptides
    Products named: Retatrutide, tirzepatide, bacteriostatic water

    "Despite statements on your product labeling marketing your products for “Research Use Only,” and “not intended for human consumption, medical use, or veterinary use,” evidence obtained from your website establishes that your products are intended to be drugs for human use."

    FDA warning letter

  4. Sep 9, 2025 · GLP-1 Solution
    Products named: Retatrutide, compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide

    "your retatrutide drug products are unapproved new drugs and misbranded drugs"

    FDA warning letter

  5. Feb 26, 2025 · USApeptide.com
    Products named: Semaglutide, tirzepatide

    "Unapproved new drugs do not carry the same assurances of safety and effectiveness as those drugs subject to FDA oversight."

    FDA warning letter

FAQ

Does "research use only" make a peptide legal to sell for people?

Not according to these letters. FDA says labels like "research use only" don't override evidence from a seller's website that products are intended for human use (see the Gram Peptides letter).

Why are there no recalls listed?

We only list items we've verified on fda.gov with a direct link. We haven't yet found an FDA recall notice specific to the peptides we cover. When we do, it goes here with its date and link.
