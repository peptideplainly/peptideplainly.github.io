Semaglutide vs tirzepatide vs retatrutide: the numbers

Semaglutide: FDA-approved Tirzepatide: FDA-approved Retatrutide: Research purposes only Checked against the published trials Oct 18, 2026

Wegovy or Zepbound? Ozempic or Mounjaro? These two are the biggest names in weight loss, and they've actually gone toe to toe in a randomized trial. Here's who won, by how much, what the landmark trials showed for each, and where the triple-agonist retatrutide fits in.

The verdict

Tirzepatide won the head-to-head.

  • −20.2% vs −13.7% average weight change at 72 weeks, tirzepatide vs semaglutide (SURMOUNT-5, 751 adults).
  • −18.4 cm vs −13.0 cm off the waist.
  • Tirzepatide users were more likely to hit every big target: at least 10%, 15%, 20% and 25% weight loss.

SURMOUNT-5: the head-to-head trial

Published in the New England Journal of Medicine in 2025 (Aronne et al., NEJM 2025, PMID 40353578). 751 adults with obesity and without type 2 diabetes were randomized 1:1 to the maximum tolerated weekly dose of tirzepatide (10 or 15 mg) or semaglutide (1.7 or 2.4 mg) for 72 weeks. It was an open-label phase 3b trial funded by Eli Lilly, the maker of tirzepatide.

Where does retatrutide fit?

Research purposes only Retatrutide is Lilly's triple agonist and the name everyone's searching next to these two. It has posted the biggest average weight-loss numbers of the three in its own trials. But it has never been tested head-to-head against semaglutide or tirzepatide, so the numbers below come from different trials with different people, lengths and dose arms. Compare loosely.

How they work

  • Semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus) activates one hormone receptor: GLP-1.
  • Tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound) activates two: GIP and GLP-1.
  • Retatrutide (investigational, Lilly) activates three: GIP, GLP-1 and glucagon.

Label dosing for weight management

  • Wegovy label: shot steps up from 0.25 mg to a usual 2.4 mg weekly; pill steps up from 1.5 mg to 25 mg daily.
  • Zepbound label: starts at 2.5 mg weekly, steps up by 2.5 mg at least every 4 weeks, maintenance 5, 10 or 15 mg.

Semaglutide vs tirzepatide FAQ

Which is better for weight loss, semaglutide or tirzepatide?

In the only head-to-head trial, SURMOUNT-5, tirzepatide won: −20.2% average weight change at 72 weeks vs −13.7% for semaglutide, in 751 adults with obesity and no type 2 diabetes.

How much weight do people lose on semaglutide?

In STEP 1, adults on semaglutide 2.4 mg weekly averaged −14.9% at 68 weeks (−15.3 kg) vs −2.4% on placebo, alongside lifestyle intervention.

How much weight do people lose on tirzepatide?

In SURMOUNT-1, average weight change at 72 weeks was −15.0% on 5 mg, −19.5% on 10 mg and −20.9% on 15 mg, vs −3.1% on placebo.

What's the difference between how they work?

Semaglutide activates one hormone receptor, GLP-1. Tirzepatide activates two, GIP and GLP-1.

Are the side effects different?

In all three trials the most common side effects were gastrointestinal, mostly mild to moderate and mainly during dose escalation (SURMOUNT) or transient (STEP 1).

Is retatrutide better than tirzepatide?

Nobody knows yet. Retatrutide's published trials (TRIUMPH-1: −25.0% on 12 mg at 80 weeks, counting dropouts) posted bigger average numbers than tirzepatide's SURMOUNT-1 (−20.9% on 15 mg at 72 weeks), but they were different trials and there's no head-to-head. Retatrutide is still investigational, with no approval date announced.
