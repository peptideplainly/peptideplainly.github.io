What are cagrilintide and CagriSema?

Cagrilintide is a long-acting version of amylin, a hormone released with insulin that helps you feel full. CagriSema is Novo Nordisk's once-weekly combination of cagrilintide 2.4 mg and semaglutide 2.4 mg, and if approved it would be the first injectable GLP-1 plus amylin combination (Novo Nordisk).

Is CagriSema FDA-approved?

Investigational, not FDA-approved. Novo Nordisk submitted CagriSema to the FDA on Dec 18, 2025 (Novo Nordisk); no decision has been announced.

Investigational, not FDA-approved. Known risks: gastrointestinal side effects in 79.6% vs 39.9% on placebo in REDEFINE 1 (NEJM, 2025); online "research" versions aren't the trial drug. How to check a lab report.

Dosage

No approved dose yet. The trials tested cagrilintide 2.4 mg plus semaglutide 2.4 mg once weekly, the strength Novo submitted to the FDA. This is Novo's fixed-dose trial product, not a guide for "research" cagrilintide.

What researchers have found so far

  • REDEFINE 1 (NEJM, 2025): 3,417 adults with obesity or overweight and no diabetes, 68 weeks. CagriSema: −20.4% average weight change vs −3.0% on placebo (treatment-policy estimand, which counts dropouts). Gastrointestinal side effects hit 79.6% vs 39.9% on placebo, mostly mild to moderate and temporary.
  • REDEFINE 2 (NEJM, 2025): 1,206 adults with type 2 diabetes, 68 weeks. −13.7% vs −3.4% on placebo (counts dropouts), and 73.5% reached an HbA1c of 6.5% or less vs 15.9%.
  • Different trials from tirzepatide's and retatrutide's, so compare loosely. See the GLP-1 comparison.

Cagrilintide & CagriSema FAQ

How much weight do people lose on CagriSema?

In REDEFINE 1, the average was −20.4% at 68 weeks vs −3.0% on placebo, counting dropouts. In people with type 2 diabetes (REDEFINE 2), −13.7% vs −3.4%.

Is CagriSema FDA-approved?

Not yet. Novo Nordisk submitted it to the FDA on Dec 18, 2025, and no decision has been announced as of Oct 18, 2026.

Can cagrilintide be compounded?

No. The FDA says cagrilintide can't be compounded under federal law.

What dose was used in trials?

The trial arm was cagrilintide 2.4 mg plus semaglutide 2.4 mg once weekly. There's no approved dose yet.
