CJC-1295 & ipamorelin: the famous GH stack, and what studies show

Research purposes only Checked against official sources Oct 16, 2026

What researchers are excited about: one CJC-1295 shot, days of higher growth hormone

In small placebo-controlled studies in healthy adults, a single CJC-1295 injection raised growth hormone 2–10× for 6+ days and IGF-1 1.5–3× for 9–11 days (J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2006). That's hormone levels, not muscle or fat.

Why people are excited

The gym crowd's favorite GH duo.

  • One CJC-1295 shot raised growth hormone 2–10× for 6+ days in healthy adults.
  • IGF-1 rose 1.5–3× for 9–11 days in the same studies.
  • Ipamorelin was well tolerated in a 114-patient hospital trial.

The highlights

  • What they are: two lab-made peptides that push the body to release growth hormone.
  • Status: research purposes only. Not FDA-approved.
  • Research: small human studies show CJC-1295 raises GH and IGF-1 for days after one shot.
  • Dosage: no established human dose.
  • Why it's popular: a big, multi-day growth-hormone pulse from a single CJC-1295 shot in studies.

What are CJC-1295 and ipamorelin?

CJC-1295 is a long-acting version of growth-hormone-releasing hormone. Ipamorelin mimics ghrelin, a hunger hormone that also triggers growth-hormone release. Online they're usually sold and talked about as a pair.

Are CJC-1295 and ipamorelin FDA-approved?

Neither is FDA-approved. Both are banned in sport (WADA).

Not FDA-approved; sold for research only. Known risks: FDA has identified serious adverse events with CJC-1295, including increased heart rate and a whole-body flushing reaction (FDA). How to check a lab report.

Dosage

There's no established human dose, so we don't list one. The human studies dosed by body weight in research settings.

What researchers have found so far

  • CJC-1295 (J Clin Endocrinol Metab, 2006): small placebo-controlled studies in healthy adults. One shot raised growth hormone 2–10× for 6+ days and IGF-1 1.5–3× for 9–11 days. These measured hormone levels, not muscle, fat or recovery.
  • Ipamorelin (Int J Colorectal Dis, 2014): a 114-patient trial after bowel surgery, given by IV in hospital. It was well tolerated but did not beat placebo on recovery of gut function.
  • The combo: we found no published human trial of CJC-1295 plus ipamorelin together.

CJC-1295 & ipamorelin FAQ

What is the CJC-1295 ipamorelin dosage?

There's no established human dose because both are research-only.

Is CJC-1295 FDA-approved?

No. It's sold for research purposes only.

Does ipamorelin work?

It triggers growth-hormone release and was well tolerated in a 114-patient hospital trial, though that trial didn't speed gut recovery after surgery.

Are CJC-1295 and ipamorelin banned in sport?

Yes. Both are named under S2.2.4 of the 2026 WADA Prohibited List, banned at all times.
