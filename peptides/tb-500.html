TB-500 (thymosin beta-4): benefits, research & what people use it for

What is TB-500?

Thymosin beta-4 (Tβ4) is a natural protein your body uses for cell movement and tissue repair. "TB-500" is the name for a synthetic product based on part of Tβ4, a favorite in recovery circles. Clinical trials so far have used Tβ4 itself.

Is TB-500 FDA-approved?

Neither TB-500 nor thymosin beta-4 is an FDA-approved drug. The 2026 WADA Prohibited List bans "thymosin-β4 and its derivatives e.g. TB-500" at all times (section S2). FDA's compounding safety page says it identified no human exposure data for the TB-500 fragment (FDA page). Its compounding status is also under review in 2026.

TB-500 dosage: what we actually know

There is no established human dose for TB-500, and FDA says it found no human exposure data for the TB-500 fragment.

  • Thymosin beta-4 (the parent protein) has been tested in people. One example is a 96-patient heart-attack trial of a recombinant version given after a stent procedure (PMID 41229390). The abstract doesn't state the dose, and the overall result wasn't significant.
  • Eye-drop versions have been developed for eye-surface conditions (PMID 30063853).

Why the numbers online don't agree

  • Many online "protocols" are converted from animal studies, where doses are set by body weight in rats or mice. Those don't translate directly to people.
  • The few human reports use different routes and settings, with no agreed standard.
  • Unregulated products can vary in what they actually contain.
  • Sellers and influencers often share numbers without sources.

  • Is there any human evidence for my specific goal, or only animal studies?
  • Could it interact with my other medicines or conditions?
  • Is there an FDA-approved option for what I'm trying to treat?
  • How would we know if it's working, or causing harm?

What the research shows

  • Heart attack trial (Cardiovasc Res, 2025, PMID 41229390): recombinant human Tβ4 was tested in 96 heart-attack patients. The overall difference in infarct size vs. placebo was not significant. A benefit was seen only in a subgroup dosed early.
  • Eye research review (Expert Opin Biol Ther, 2018, PMID 30063853): describes Tβ4 eye drops being developed for corneal and dry-eye conditions. This is a researcher's narrative review, not trial results.
  • We found no controlled human trials of products sold as "TB-500."

TB-500 FAQ

What is the TB-500 dosage?

There's no established human dose because it's research-only.

Is TB-500 the same as thymosin beta-4?

No. TB-500 is a synthetic product based on part of thymosin beta-4. Clinical studies used thymosin beta-4 itself.

Is TB-500 legal for athletes?

No. The 2026 WADA Prohibited List bans thymosin beta-4 and its derivatives, including TB-500, at all times.

Does TB-500 work?

Research on thymosin beta-4, the parent protein, shows repair activity, including a benefit in early-dosed heart-attack patients. Controlled trials of TB-500 itself haven't been published yet.
