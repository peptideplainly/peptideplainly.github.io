Retatrutide release date & FDA approval status

Investigational: not approved; only available in Lilly clinical trials Not FDA-approved

Retatrutide has racked up phase 3 win after phase 3 win, with average weight loss up to −25.0% in its published TRIUMPH-1 results. So when can you actually get it? Here's exactly where it stands, with every date sourced.

Where it stands right now

  • Not FDA-approved. Lilly says retatrutide is investigational and can't be legally sold or marketed for human use.
  • Filing: Lilly says it plans to submit to the FDA for U.S. approval in Q1 2027.
  • Release date: none announced. Lilly hasn't given an approval or launch date, so we don't predict one.
  • Five positive phase 3 studies, according to Lilly, and it says it has the clinical data to support global submissions for obesity, knee osteoarthritis pain and sleep apnea.

The TRIUMPH timeline

  1. 2023Phase 2 published

    Retatrutide 12 mg averaged −24.2% at 48 weeks vs −2.1% on placebo in 338 adults (NEJM 2023, PMID 37366315).

  2. May–Aug 2023TRIUMPH phase 3 trials start

    ClinicalTrials.gov start dates: TRIUMPH-3 May 30, TRIUMPH-1 July 10, TRIUMPH-2 July 11, TRIUMPH-4 Aug 1, 2023 (NCT05882045, NCT05929066, NCT05929079, NCT05931367). Lilly says the initial program enrolled more than 5,800 people.

  3. Dec 11, 2025TRIUMPH-4: first phase 3 win

    Obesity plus knee osteoarthritis, 68 weeks. 12 mg: −28.7% average weight (efficacy estimand; −23.7% treatment-regimen) and knee pain down 4.5 points on WOMAC at 9 mg (Lilly).

  4. May 21, 2026TRIUMPH-1 topline

    Obesity without diabetes, 80 weeks. 12 mg: −28.3% (efficacy estimand; −25.0% treatment-regimen), and 45.3% lost at least 30% of body weight (Lilly).

  5. July 23, 2026TRIUMPH-2 & TRIUMPH-3 topline, filing plan

    Type 2 diabetes: up to −20.8%. Severe obesity with heart disease: up to −22.6% (efficacy estimand, 80 weeks). Lilly said it plans to submit a BLA to FDA in Q1 2027 (Lilly).

  6. Sept 29, 2026Full results published

    TRIUMPH-1 in NEJM: −17.6%, −23.7% and −25.0% vs −3.9% on placebo (PMID 42814954). TRIUMPH-2 in The Lancet: −11.9%, −16.8% and −18.8% vs −5.1% (PMID 42810372). Both treatment-regimen estimand, online Sept 29.

  7. Oct 2, 2026FDA: not a biologic

    On remand from a federal court, FDA again concluded retatrutide is not a biological product, so an application would need to be submitted under section 505 of the FD&C Act rather than as a BLA (FDA letter, filed in the Seventh Circuit Oct 8, 2026).

"Efficacy estimand" estimates the effect if everyone stayed on treatment; "treatment-regimen estimand" counts everyone randomized, including people who stopped. That's why Lilly's press-release numbers run higher than the published ones.

What happens next

  • Submission: Lilly says it is finishing the chemistry, manufacturing and controls (CMC) package and then plans to submit in Q1 2027.
  • Pathway fight: Lilly wants retatrutide reviewed as a biologic (BLA). FDA's October 2 letter says it's a drug that needs a section 505 application. Lilly's legal challenge is ongoing in the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit.
  • More trials: Lilly is also studying retatrutide in type 2 diabetes, chronic low back pain, cardiovascular and kidney outcomes, and fatty liver disease (MASLD).

Can you get it now?

Not legitimately. The only real retatrutide is Lilly's product in clinical trials. Products sold online are labeled for research purposes only, and their content isn't checked. Retatrutide release date FAQ

When will retatrutide be released?

There's no release date yet. Lilly has said it plans to submit retatrutide to the FDA for U.S. approval in the first quarter of 2027. Lilly hasn't announced an approval or launch date, and we don't guess one.

Is retatrutide FDA-approved?

No. Retatrutide is investigational. Lilly says it cannot be legally sold or marketed for human use.

Has Lilly filed retatrutide with the FDA?

Not as of Lilly's latest update we found (July 23, 2026). Lilly said it was completing the manufacturing and quality (CMC) data package and planned to submit in Q1 2027.

Will retatrutide be reviewed as a drug or a biologic?

That's in dispute. Lilly plans a Biologics License Application (BLA). In an October 2, 2026 letter, FDA again concluded retatrutide is not a biological product and that an application would need to go under section 505 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. Lilly's legal challenge is ongoing.

How much weight did people lose in the retatrutide trials?

In TRIUMPH-1, published in NEJM, average weight change at 80 weeks was −17.6% (4 mg), −23.7% (9 mg) and −25.0% (12 mg) vs −3.9% on placebo.
