Semaglutide vs tirzepatide vs retatrutide vs orforglipron

These four are rewriting weight loss. Trials have shown average drops of 11% to 25% of body weight, and you can now pick a weekly shot or a daily pill. Here's how the big four stack up, with every number linked to its source.

  • Biggest average result: retatrutide, −25.0% in TRIUMPH-1 (still in trials).
  • Strongest approved drug: tirzepatide (Zepbound), −20.9% in SURMOUNT-1, and it beat semaglutide head-to-head.
  • The original: semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy), now as a shot or a pill, with heart protection shown in SELECT.
  • Easiest pill: orforglipron (Foundayo), once a day with or without food.

GLP-1 comparison FAQ

Which GLP-1 causes the most weight loss?

On average, retatrutide posted the biggest number: −25.0% at 80 weeks on 12 mg in TRIUMPH-1. But it's still investigational. Among approved drugs, tirzepatide leads: −20.9% at 72 weeks on 15 mg in SURMOUNT-1, and it beat semaglutide head-to-head in SURMOUNT-5 (−20.2% vs −13.7%).

Is there a GLP-1 weight-loss pill?

Yes, two. The Wegovy pill (semaglutide 25 mg, approved December 2025) averaged −13.6% at 64 weeks in OASIS 4. Foundayo (orforglipron, approved April 2026) averaged up to −11.2% at 72 weeks in ATTAIN-1, and its label says it can be taken with or without food.

Is retatrutide FDA-approved?

Not yet. As of October 2026 it's investigational. Lilly reported positive Phase 3 results and plans to submit it to the FDA in early 2027. There's no approved human dose.

What's the difference between semaglutide and tirzepatide?

Semaglutide acts on one hormone receptor (GLP-1). Tirzepatide acts on two (GIP and GLP-1). In the SURMOUNT-5 head-to-head trial, tirzepatide produced more average weight loss at 72 weeks.

What's the difference between Ozempic and Wegovy?

Both are semaglutide from Novo Nordisk. Ozempic (shot or tablet) is approved for type 2 diabetes. Wegovy (shot or pill) is approved for weight management and heart-risk reduction, with its own doses.

Is orforglipron a peptide?

No. Orforglipron is a small-molecule drug that hits the same GLP-1 receptor as semaglutide. That's part of why it can be a simple daily pill.

Can you compare these trial numbers directly?

Only loosely. Each trial had different people, doses and lengths (64 to 80 weeks). The only head-to-head trial among these four is SURMOUNT-5, tirzepatide vs semaglutide.
