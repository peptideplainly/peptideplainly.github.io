About Peptide Plainly

Peptide Plainly is 13 plain-English peptide guides plus a free newsletter that lands every Friday. The newsletter turns new peptide research from PubMed into short, plain-language summaries. For each study we cover what was studied, what was found, the study type and size, why it matters, and its limitations.

How we write summaries

Summaries are based only on the published abstract, and every summary links to the original paper. We never add findings that aren't in the source.

Medical disclaimer

Peptide Plainly is not medical advice. Content is for general educational and informational purposes only. It summarizes scientific research and isn't a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. We don't sell anything ourselves. We partner with lab-tested vendors, including research-purposes-only suppliers, and every partnership is clearly disclosed. Research-only compounds are sold for research purposes only, not for human use. We never provide personal dosing guidance. For FDA-approved drugs we only summarize the official label so you can follow along with your prescriber. Many compounds discussed in research are not approved by the FDA or other regulators for human use. Early-stage, animal, and small studies often don't hold up in larger human trials. Summaries may contain errors. Always consult the original paper and a licensed healthcare professional before making any health decision.
