About Peptide Plainly

Peptides, plainly. No BS. This is the most exciting moment peptides have ever had, and we're here for it: {N} guides to the peptides everyone's talking about, plus a free Friday newsletter with the week's biggest peptide research.

We cover what's hot, from GLP-1s like retatrutide and tirzepatide to recovery, skin, growth-hormone and longevity peptides, and we lead with the real results. Every claim links to the study or label behind it, and we always say when a result comes from a small study, an animal study or cells in a lab.

How we're different

  • Status, dated. Every guide shows when its FDA status was last checked, and our FDA status tracker logs each change with its source.
  • Enforcement, quoted. We track FDA warning letters to peptide sellers word for word, linked to fda.gov.
  • Lab reports, explained. Our guide to checking a lab report covers COAs and FDA's own lookup tools.
  • Every study labeled. We say when a result comes from a small study, an animal study or lab cells, and every guide card shows how many sources it cites.

How we write summaries

Newsletter summaries are based on the published abstract, and every one links to the original paper. We never add findings that aren't in the source.

How we work with sponsors

We partner with lab-tested vendors, including research-only suppliers with independent third-party COAs. Sponsored content is always labeled, and sponsors never change what our guides say. See Advertise.

Medical disclaimer

Not medical advice. Education only. We never give personal dosing guidance: for FDA-approved drugs we summarize the official label, and research-only compounds are sold for research, not human use. Summaries may contain errors, so check the original paper and talk to a licensed clinician before making health decisions.
